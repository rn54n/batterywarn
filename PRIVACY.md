# Privacy Policy

BatteryWarn does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.**
- **No network connections.** The app never contacts a server. The Mac App Store version runs in the App Sandbox without network access.
- **Battery information stays on your Mac.** BatteryWarn reads the battery level, the charging state and the time remaining from macOS to decide when to show the warning. Nothing of it leaves your Mac.
- **Settings are stored locally.** Your warning level, the dismiss option, launch at login and the appearance are saved in the app's preferences on your Mac.

The links in the Settings window open in your web browser. What happens on those websites is covered by their own privacy policies.
